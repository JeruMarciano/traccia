# v0.2 bundle measurement — what document ingestion cost

**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Commit:** the v0.2 branch at document-ingestion completion
**Host:** macOS, aarch64, `npm run tauri build`

## The numbers

| Artefact | Bytes |
|---|---|
| Electron `Traccia-1.0.0-arm64.dmg` (Phase 1, for comparison) | 99,452,988 |
| v0.1 macOS `.dmg` (2026-08-02) | 1,647,339 |
| **v0.2 macOS `Traccia_1.0.0_aarch64.dmg`** | **2,721,163** |

Delta against v0.1: **+1,073,824 bytes (+65.2%)** — see the amendment at the end, which accounts for the last 422,632 of it. Against Electron: still **36.5× smaller**.

Windows: to be read from the `report the shipped size` step of `bundle (windows-latest)` on this
branch's CI run, as in the v0.1 document. Not estimated here.

## Where the bytes went

All of it is the four parser crates, and most of that is `lopdf`. Measured on the release
binary: 2,652,608 → 3,844,608 (+1,192,000, +45%); the `.dmg` compresses that to +649,476.

- `lopdf` — the dominant share. It links an unconditional crypto stack (aes, cbc, sha2, md-5,
  rc4, and their support crates) for encrypted PDFs. **Checked for a trim and there is none:**
  lopdf 0.44's `[features]` are chrono/jiff/rayon/time/serde/image/ttf-parser/async — all
  already off via `default-features = false` — and encryption support carries no feature gate.
  The alternatives were weighed in the T1 report and none is a real substitute.
- `calamine` (XLSX), `zip` (DOCX container), `quick-xml` (DOCX text, already transitive via
  tauri → plist on macOS) are comparatively small.

## The trade, stated

PDF is the format a client is most likely to hand over in a first checkup, so paying ~600 KB of
download for it is the right side of the trade — the app remains under 2.3 MB, an order of
magnitude inside the 10–15 MB the Tauri port was justified by, and two orders inside Electron.
Recorded here so that if the size constraint ever tightens, the first place to look is PDF
support, and the second is a lighter PDF text extractor.

## Licences

Sweep re-run after adding all four crates: no new copyleft. The only copyleft in the tree
remains the five MPL-2.0 crates inherited from Tauri and recorded in `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md`;
`lightningcss` (MPL-2.0) stays build-time only.

## Amended 2026-08-02, after the security audit

The figure above was re-measured after the audit's Critical finding forced `[profile.release]`
from `panic = "abort"` to `panic = "unwind"` — without unwinding, `extract.rs`'s `catch_unwind`
around a hostile document's parse was inert and one panic in a parser crate killed the app.

Unwind tables cost **+422,632 bytes** on the `.dmg` (2,298,531 → 2,721,163) and +944,832 on the
binary. Paid without hesitation: the alternative was a documented crash-safety guarantee that
did not exist in the shipped artefact. It also closes a v0.1 carry — `Drop` now runs on a panic,
so a panicked scan no longer orphans headless Chrome or leaves a client's cookies in a temp
profile directory.

Final v0.2 macOS figure: **2,721,163 bytes**, 36.5× smaller than the Electron build.
